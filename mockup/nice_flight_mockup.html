Nice Flight! — screen mockup, draft 3

X14 full-screen widget canvas (640×360), FS1–FS4 labels pinned to the top row as in Throw Trainer. X20-class radios get the same layout scaled from measured text height plus tap on the key labels. Badge icons are emoji stand-ins for shipped PNG icons (Ethos fonts have no emoji). Draft 3, 2026-09-16: your draft 2 annotations plus every decision from the badges walk in badges_screen.html.

Mid-flight badge alert. The moment a badge is earned in the air (Peak or Duration crossing a rung, a record falling, a yo-yo, rebound or save completing) the radio vibrates and a large dark pill appears at bottom right, the same pill as the highlighted badge on the recap, sized for reading at arm's length, with "Badge earned" right-aligned in small type directly above it, in the right column under Launch, so it costs no vertical space on the left. The second frame is the record variant in gold. It stays for the rest of the flight; a later badge replaces it, so the pill always shows the latest one earned. The stacked lines at bottom left stay put. Vibration only, no sound, so it never talks over the vario. CFG Alerts = Off silences this too, pill included. Hat trick cannot be earned in the air; it waits for the recap.

6 · CFG

Same editor pattern as Throw Trainer's CFG. Units are the widget's own setting; stored values are always ft, and Units = m switches every ladder to a round metric ladder. The climb bar is gone, so its setting is gone. One Alerts toggle covers everything: the landing sound and vibration, and the mid-flight badge vibration and pill. Off means silent and no pill; badges are still recorded and shown on the recap. Record floors (400 ft, 10 min) and the save rule (under 60 ft, climb out to 300) are fixed, not settings.

8 · Edge-case pass

Decided means I'll build it that way unless you object. Ask means I need your call.

  1. decidedRelaunch while the recap is up. The launch closes the recap and starts Live; the recap is saved and reachable in History.
  2. newBrake in the air ends the flight (Poker's known gap). SF11 zeroes Altitude at launch, so landing is accepted only under CFG "Landing needs altitude under" (default 50 ft). If telemetry is lost, the brake alone is trusted.
  3. decidedLanded without braking. The next MOM_LAUNCH release closes the previous flight; duration is the last sample above 20 ft.
  4. decidedTelemetry lost mid-flight. Timer keeps running, samples are skipped, the graph draws straight across the gap. The live screen shows dashes for Now and Max until the link returns.
  5. decidedPower-cycle or model switch mid-flight. The flight is dropped, nothing written.
  6. decidedQualification is judged at landing. The recap never appears while airborne.
  7. decidedRecords start at a floor, 400 ft and 10 minutes, so the first flight over the floor is a record and earns a badge. Replaces the earlier "first nice flight sets the record silently" rule.
  8. decidedHat trick counting. On the recap of the 5th, 10th, 15th nice flight of the day, by the radio's local date.
  9. decidedMemory on long flights. 1 Hz samples capped at 60 min plus a running turning-point tracker.
  10. decidedStorage. Files/flights.csv one row per throw, Files/badges.csv one row per family (firsts, tally, best, history), Files/config.csv as in Throw Trainer. Everything in badges.csv is recomputable from flights.csv.
  11. decidedMetric ladders. Separate round ladders when Units = m.
  12. decidedIdle shows the last throw honestly, fumble or not.
  13. decidedGraph points. Start, landing, and up to six reversals of at least the climb size; the smallest reversals are dropped first.
  14. decidedWhere Nice Flight! lives on the radio. Its own main-screen page, reached with the page key; Throw Trainer keeps its page. Detection and logging run whether or not the page is showing.
  15. decidedMid-flight badges make the flight nice. Any badge earned in the air (a 3× 100 ft yo-yo under 300 ft is the one case that can happen on an otherwise ordinary flight) marks the flight as nice, so it gets the recap and appears in History. A badge without a recap would be lost.
  16. decidedMid-flight badge alert timing. Peak and Record height fire when the max first crosses the line; Duration and Record time when the timer does; yo-yo, rebound and save the moment their last condition is met. A power-cycle after an alert but before landing still drops the flight, badge included, per case 5.
  17. decidedNice flight alert. A couple of seconds after a nice flight lands, the radio plays an audible alert and vibrates, so you know a recap is waiting even with another page up. CFG "Alerts" turns this and the mid-flight badge alert off together. Ordinary flights stay silent.
