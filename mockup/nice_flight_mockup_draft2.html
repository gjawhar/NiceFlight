Nice Flight! — screen mockup, draft 2

X14 full-screen widget canvas (640×360), FS1–FS4 labels pinned to the top row as in Throw Trainer. X20-class radios get the same layout scaled from measured text height plus tap on the key labels. Badge icons are emoji here as stand-ins: Ethos fonts have no emoji, so the radio draws shipped PNG icons of the same design (the same mechanism as the widget icons already in use). Draft 2, 2026-09-16, incorporating your annotations on draft 1.

Timer and max side by side, equal weight. Current altitude and launch height underneath. Bottom strip is the climb/sink bar, green up / red down, intensity = rate, no deadband, grey when telemetry is lost. Keys are inert in flight.

2 · Recap — after a qualifying flight

Shown when a flight beating 300 ft OR 5:00 (CFG) lands; stays until DISMISS or the next launch. Curve is the simplified trace (start, launch peak, landing, every reversal ≥ climb size, max 6 kept). Highs get green pills, lows red, matching the other two apps' good/bad colours. Time above launch and climbs are separate fields now. The rotary moves a highlight across the badges and the caption line under them reads that badge's definition, so the recap explains itself without leaving it.

8 · Badge gallery — every badge at display size

Each card: the badge as it appears on the recap, its greyed unearned form, the plain-words definition (the same text the caption line shows), and alternates for the icon. Icons are emoji stand-ins; on the radio they become 24 px PNGs (Noto or Twemoji, both open-licensed) drawn with lcd.drawBitmap, so whatever you pick here can be shipped as-is.

🏔️Peak 400🏔️Peak 1300🏔️Peak 2000
Peak. The highest altitude the flight reached. Firsts at 400, 600, 800, 1000, 1250, 1500, 2000 ft. Every flight over 1000 ft earns one, numbered to the hundred below its max.
Alternates: ⛰️🦅🎈
⏱️10 min⏱️16 min⏱️30 min
Duration. Whole minutes airborne. Firsts at 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 min. Every flight of 15 min or more earns one showing its minutes.
Alternates: ⌛🕰️🐢
🪀Yo-yo 200🪀Yo-yo 400🪀Yo-yo 500
Yo-yo. Three or more climbs in one flight. The number is the biggest climb size all three managed: Yo-yo 200 means three separate climbs of at least 200 ft. Firsts per 100 ft; every flight at 300 or more earns one.
Question: does the launch itself count as one of the three climbs? Draft says no, climbs are counted after the launch peak.
Alternates: 🎢〰️🐬
🏀Rebound 300🏀Rebound 1000🏀Rebound —
Rebound. You sank at least 75% of the way down from a high, then climbed back over it. The number is that high, to the hundred. Only counts from 300 ft up. Earned every time.
Question: 300 → 75 → 300 is the definition you gave. Does back to 90% (270 ft) count, or must it be over 300? Draft says over.
Alternates: 🦘🔁🏐
🛟Save 55🛟Save 22🛟Save —
Low save. You got below 100 ft and still climbed back to 400. The number is the lowest point you saved from, exact. Lower is better. Earned every time.
Alternates: 🆘🪂🧗😅
🎩Hat trick🎩Hat trick ×2🎩Hat trick
Hat trick. Five nice flights in one day. Ten is two, fifteen is three. Awarded on the recap of the 5th, 10th, 15th nice flight.
Question: on the recap it reads "Hat trick" with no number, and "×2" on the second one that day. OK?
Alternates: 🎯🔥🖐️
🏆PB 588 ft🏆PB 16:05
Personal best. Two badges, height and time. Earned every time a flight beats your own record, showing the exact new record. Gold border so they stand out from the tiered badges. The very first nice flight sets the records silently.
Question: one 🏆 for both with the number telling them apart, or two icons (say 🥇 height, ⏳ time)?
Alternates: 🥇⭐🚀
Reading a badge, the rule. Icon says the family, number says how much. No number means the family has no size (hat trick). Grey means not earned yet. Gold border means a record. The caption line always spells out the highlighted badge, on the recap and on the badges screen, so nobody has to memorise the icons.

9 · Edge-case pass

Decided means I'll build it that way unless you object. Ask means I need your call.

  1. decidedRelaunch while the recap is up. The launch closes the recap and starts Live immediately; the recap is already saved and reachable in History.
  2. newBrake in the air ends the flight (Poker's known gap). Since SF11 zeroes Altitude at launch, ground reads near 0, so landing is accepted only when altitude is under CFG "Landing needs altitude under" (default 50 ft). Above that the brake is ignored. If telemetry is lost, the brake alone is trusted.
  3. decidedLanded without braking. Poker's rule: the next MOM_LAUNCH release closes the previous flight. Duration is taken as the last sample above 20 ft.
  4. decidedTelemetry lost mid-flight. Bar goes grey, timer keeps running, samples are skipped, the graph draws straight across the gap.
  5. decidedPower-cycle or model switch mid-flight. The flight is dropped, nothing written.
  6. decidedQualification is judged at landing. The recap never appears while airborne.
  7. decidedFirst nice flight ever sets both PB records silently and earns no PB badge.
  8. decidedHat trick counting. On the recap of the 5th, 10th, 15th nice flight of the day, by the radio's local date.
  9. decidedClimb-rate source for the bar. The VSpeed sensor already on your model; fallback altitude delta over the last second.
  10. decidedMemory on long flights. 1 Hz samples capped at 60 min, plus a running turning-point tracker so longer flights still get a correct graph and stats.
  11. decidedStorage. Files/flights.csv one row per throw, Files/badges.csv one row per family, Files/config.csv as in Throw Trainer. Firsts and PBs are recomputable from flights.csv.
  12. decidedMetric ladders. Separate round ladders when Units = m: Peak 120/180/250/300/400/450/600 m, Duration unchanged, climb size 30 m, nice-flight height 100 m, landing 15 m, low save under 30 m back to 120 m, rebound from 100 m.
  13. decidedIdle shows the last throw honestly, fumble or not.
  14. askWhere Nice Flight! lives on the radio, plainly. The X14 main screen has several pages you flip through with the page key (like swiping between home screens on a phone). A full-screen widget takes a whole page. Throw Trainer already takes one. So Nice Flight! goes on its own page and you flip to it. Live and Idle only draw when their page is showing, but detection and logging run regardless, because Ethos calls a widget's wakeup even when it's not on screen (Throw Trainer relies on this already). Two things to confirm: is flipping pages acceptable, and should the recap get a sound or vibration cue so you know it's there when Throw Trainer's page is up?
