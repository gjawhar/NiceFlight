Badges screen — Record height highlighted

"PB" is gone; the family is called Record. Tile: bold is the record itself, small line is how many times you've beaten it, which is the number of Record badges you hold. The row is the record's history, newest first, each with its date; beaten records get one diagonal slash corner to corner, which marks them without covering the digits, and the standing one is plain. Records start at a floor, 400 ft and 10 minutes, so the first flight over the floor is itself a record and earns a badge. Sample: the height record has fallen four times since the floor.
